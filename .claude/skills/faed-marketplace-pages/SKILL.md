# Faed Marketplace Pages

## Goal

Give Faed a consistent public marketplace experience that feels intentionally designed rather than generated page-by-page.

## Home page

The first viewport must immediately answer:
- what Faed is;
- why prices are lower;
- that sellers are verified merchants;
- that retail and wholesale can both exist;
- where to start browsing.

Recommended section order:
1. hero with clear value proposition + strong CTA;
2. featured inventory;
3. category navigation;
4. how Faed works;
5. condition / discount transparency explanation;
6. merchant trust section;
7. merchant acquisition CTA.

Do not fill the page with generic marketing fluff.

## Shop / browse page

Must support:
- search;
- category filtering;
- condition filtering;
- discount reason filtering;
- price filtering;
- brand/size/color when applicable;
- sort;
- clear active filters.

Mobile behavior:
- filter drawer/offcanvas;
- visible sort;
- clear filter count.

## Category page

Should feel like a focused browse surface, not a duplicate of Home.
Emphasize:
- category context;
- filters;
- product grid;
- related subcategories if useful.

## Listing detail page

This page decides trust and conversion.

It must answer user questions in order:
1. Is this the product I want?
2. Why is it cheaper?
3. What exactly is its condition?
4. Can I see the issue if one exists?
5. Is the merchant trustworthy?
6. Is my size/color available?
7. How do I receive it?
8. Can I buy wholesale if needed?

Design the page around those questions.

## Merchant store page

Purpose:
- browse one merchant's Faed inventory;
- understand the merchant identity and trust.

Include:
- merchant name;
- verified state;
- short merchant context;
- inventory list;
- filters if enough inventory exists.

Do not turn it into a highly custom branded microsite.

## Search and empty states

Every discovery page must handle:
- no results;
- sold-out inventory;
- filtered-out results;
- missing merchant inventory.

Empty states should help recovery:
- clear filters;
- go back to categories;
- continue shopping.

## Cross-page consistency

Keep these components visually and behaviorally consistent:
- product cards;
- condition blocks;
- price blocks;
- verification indicators;
- filters;
- empty states;
- CTA styling.
