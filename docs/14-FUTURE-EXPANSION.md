# 14 — Future Expansion

Faed is multi-sector by product vision, but expansion must preserve a similar operating model.

## Phase A — Launch
Fashion Overstock:
- Clothing
- Shoes
- Bags & Accessories

Core capabilities:
- options/variants;
- condition;
- discount reasons;
- B2C;
- B2B;
- merchant fulfillment;
- trust.

---

## Phase B — Non-electrical Home Supplies

Examples:
- kitchenware;
- storage/organization;
- linens;
- lightweight home accessories.

Likely additions:
- dimensions;
- weight;
- fragility attribute;
- bundles.

The same condition, B2C/B2B, merchant-verification, and fulfillment model largely remains valid.

---

## Phase C — Small Home Appliances

Examples:
- coffee machines;
- blenders;
- air fryers;
- small vacuums.

Required maturity before expansion:
- serial/model identifiers;
- operational test evidence;
- warranty representation;
- richer accessories checklist;
- stronger dispute tooling;
- optional inspection partner model later.

Do not enter this sector merely by adding a Category row if trust/inspection requirements are not ready.

---

## Later candidates
- Tools
- Lighting/electrical accessories
- Office equipment
- Sports supplies

Each sector requires a readiness review.

---

## Sectors excluded from near-term expansion

Do not treat these as simple category additions:
- food;
- medicine/medical products;
- highly regulated goods;
- dangerous goods;
- categories requiring a fundamentally different safety chain.

---

## Multi-sector architecture principle

Future expansion should require:
- new taxonomy;
- new category attributes;
- possibly new validation policies;

but should **not** require rewriting:
- merchant identity;
- B2C order core;
- B2B negotiation core;
- inventory concurrency;
- reviews;
- disputes;
- analytics foundation.
